      BBC News Channel Launches Live on YouTube in the UK and U.S.

      Oct 15, 2026

      BBC News is now streaming 24/7 live on YouTube in both the U.S. and the UK, building on successful live launches in Australia, Kenya, Nigeria, and Poland.

      YouTube is the home for high-quality, trusted news: in the first half of 2026 alone, viewers watched over 18 billion hours of news content on the platform.

      For viewers, live streaming brings the world closer. Whether it is breaking news, exclusive interviews, or major special events, live broadcasting lets news organizations connect with audiences in real-time. By using YouTube's live tools, iconic broadcasters like the BBC can build deep, authentic rapport, bringing global communities together to learn, discuss, and follow history as it happens.

      This partnership shows how media leaders are thriving today: by putting their heritage on an open, accessible video platform, they meet younger audiences exactly where they are.

      YouTube Music adds new labels for AI music

      Oct 14, 2026

      Generative AI is opening up new ways for artists to create, experiment, and share music. As these tools evolve, our listeners have told us they want transparency when they’re hearing an AI-generated track. Starting today, YouTube Music is rolling out labels to make it easier to see when a song was created primarily using generative AI. For example, this includes using a generative model to create the composition and vocals. These new music labels will also expand to the main YouTube app.

      Clear labels right on the player

      When you listen to a track on YouTube Music where AI played a significant role, you’ll see a label directly on the player page. Tapping the label opens a simple menu that shares more information.

      We’re teaming up with music partners and using our own detection tools to identify AI-generated tracks, applying the same labeling principles already used across YouTube. Our goal is to make it as easy as possible for viewers and listeners to have the right information and clarity when it comes to the music they listen to.

      You can find more details on disclosure guidelines and standards at the YouTube Help Center.

      Discover your next favorite video with Ask YouTube, available on web, mobile, and TVs

      By Emily Moxley, VP, Product Management, Viewer AI

      Oct 12, 2026

      First announced at Google I/O, we launched Ask YouTube on search to help viewers get to the right video faster. Now, we’re bringing this feature to mobile devices, making Ask YouTube available to U.S. viewers across web, mobile and TVs.

      This reimagined, conversational search tool allows users to ask complex queries, refine with follow up questions, and receive results in an interactive format, whether they’re on the go or using voice commands from the couch.

      We’ve already seen users ask how to DIY their Halloween costumes, dive into video essays, and debate cutting their own bangs.

      Ask YouTube on the watch page, which launched last year, is also expanding to help viewers connect even more deeply with the content and creators they love.

      Earlier this year, we added the feature to TVs, and viewers will soon be able to use Ask YouTube directly from the watch page while watching Shorts and live streams, receiving instant context as the action unfolds.

      Dr. Garth talks healthy digital habits with Mummy & Daddy Pig from Peppa Pig

      Sep 03, 2026

      As families dive headfirst into the back-to-school routine, many parents are asking a familiar question: how do we build healthy screen habits that actually stick?

      Whether you’re trying to transition from a favorite show to the dinner table without causing a muddy puddle of tears, or just trying to find good quality content, getting the right balance can feel like a whole operation.

      To help families navigate those moments, Dr. Garth Graham — a practicing physician, public health expert, father of four, and Head of YouTube Health — sat down for a very special video call with two of the world’s most recognisable parents: Mummy Pig and Daddy Pig.

      Debuting on the Peppa Pig: Muddy Puddle Parenting YouTube channel, this lighthearted sit-down unpacks practical, clinician-tested tips for managing screen time at home … no snorting required.
